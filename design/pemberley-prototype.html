A Conversation at

Pemberley

Speak with the characters of Pride and Prejudice
You enquire
What did you first think of Mr. Darcy?
Elizabeth Bennet replies
I confess I took a most decided dislike to him upon our first acquaintance. I thought him proud, disagreeable, and above being pleased. He danced but little, and slighted me at the assembly with a coldness I did not soon forget. Vol. I, Ch. 3 It was such a spur to one's wit, to have a dislike of that kind. Vol. II, Ch. 40
You address Mr. Darcy
Darcy, and what did you make of her?
Fitzwilliam Darcy replies
I own that at first I looked on her only with a critical eye, and pronounced her tolerable, but not handsome enough to tempt me. Vol. I, Ch. 3 Yet I had not long been in her company before I found myself caught by the fine expression of her dark eyes, and the easy playfulness of her manner. My objections I need not name. Vol. I, Ch. 6
